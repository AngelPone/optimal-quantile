\section*{Experiment setting}

The experiment uses a rolling fixed window of \(52\times 7\times 3=1092\)
days to fit an ETS model for each of the 154 series in the reduced M5
hierarchy.  Each forecast origin produces a 28-day base forecast.  The final
forecast origin is held out as the test set; its 28 future observations are
never used to train the reconciliation models.  For every reconciliation
problem, the last \(28\times 3=84\) historical forecast origins form the
validation set, and all earlier eligible origins form the training set.  The
in-sample experiment therefore uses 765 training origins and 84 validation
origins.  At the four out-of-sample anchor horizons, the corresponding
training-set sizes are 765, 758, 751, and 744 origins; every anchor retains
84 origins for validation.  The reconciliation matrix is initialized with the shrinkage estimator,
\(G_{\mathrm{init}}=G_{\mathrm{shr}}\).

Two residual constructions are considered.  The \emph{in-sample} residuals
are the one-step fitted residuals from each ETS training window.  Their
distribution parameters therefore vary across forecast origins.  A single
reconciliation model is trained using the one-step-ahead problem and is
applied to all 28 test horizons.  The \emph{out-of-sample} residuals are the
historical realized forecast errors \(y_{t+h}-\widehat y_{t+h\mid t}\), fitted
separately for each horizon \(h=1,\ldots,28\).  Reconciliation models are
trained at horizons \(h\in\{1,8,15,22\}\) and applied respectively to the test
horizon blocks \(1{:}7\), \(8{:}14\), \(15{:}21\), and \(22{:}28\).

For both residual constructions, two marginal distributions are evaluated.
The Normal specification uses the residual mean and standard deviation as
its location and scale.  The SkewStudentT specification uses the same
location and scale and estimates the skewness parameter \(\xi\) and degrees
of freedom \(\nu\) by maximum likelihood.  Monte Carlo residual draws are
added to the ETS point forecasts before reconciliation.  Reconciliation
training uses 3000 draws for
\(\alpha\in\{0.005,0.025,0.975,0.995\}\) and 500 draws for the remaining
quantiles; validation and test evaluation use 5000 and 10000 draws,
respectively.  Results are reported at
\(\alpha\in\{0.005,0.025,0.165,0.25,0.5,0.75,0.835,0.975,0.995\}\).
